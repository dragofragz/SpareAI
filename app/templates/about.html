Sentence-BERT (SBERT) Embeddings

Traditional keyword search fails when vehicle owners use colloquial terms (e.g. “thudding when turning over bumps” vs “worn control arm bushing”). We deploy the lightweight all-MiniLM-L6-v2 Sentence-BERT transformer model to transform unstructured text into 384-dimensional dense semantic representations where synonymous fault descriptions map to adjacent coordinates in vector space.

Case-Based Reasoning (CBR)

Instead of treating repair prediction as a black-box classification, our CBR engine retrieves the top-k most similar historical repair cases using vector cosine similarity. We apply a domain-specific affinity boost (+0.03 for make match, +0.05 for model match) to prioritize vehicle-specific mechanical nuances.

Similarity-Weighted Voting & Explainability

Top retrieved cases vote for their respective solution spare part, weighted by their cosine similarity scores. The user is presented not only with the recommendation, but also with verbatim snippets of the past repair cases that drove the decision (“why this was recommended”), establishing transparent AI explainability.

Unified Multi-Supplier & Service Network

When a local service center or workshop faces stockouts, the platform scans nearby distributor inventories, calculates great-circle road distances using the Haversine formula, and allows one-click dispatch either to the customer’s doorstep or directly to a designated workshop for installation.
